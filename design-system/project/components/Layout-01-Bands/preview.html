Page bands

Every signed-in page is a stack of full-width bands. Neighbouring bands take different tones, so the eye can tell where one part of a page ends and the next begins. The idea came from prolithica.com; only existing tokens are used.

2 · Tones
ToneColourUse
Lead · maroonaccent, a soft light from the top leftRelationships pages lead with it: organisations, people, deals.
Lead · inkink (dark theme: surface), a maroon glow top rightWork pages lead with it: Today, projects.
Lead · tintaccent-soft at 70%, hairline of accent at 10% belowWorkspace pages lead with it: team, flags, settings, notifications.
Plainbg, no borderEvery other band, starting with the one after the lead.
Softfill at 60%, separator hairlines above and belowAlternates with plain, so neighbours always differ.

The lead tone tells you which part of the app you are in. Inside a maroon or ink band the shared roles turn white-on-dark and status colours take their dark-theme values. After the lead, tones alternate by position among the bands actually shown.

3 · The number tile

Open dealsKSh 240k1 deal on the table
Won this monthKSh 310kClosed and agreed

Label, figure, one line of help. The one number that matters most on a page leads: a solid tile in the opposite tone and a bigger figure; its label names the status, so it drops the status colour. A status colour on any other figure always has its word in the help. On a phone the help line is hidden visually but still read out.

5 · Motion
ArriveEach band rises 10px and fades in over 240ms, ease-out, 60ms after the one above.
RevealBands below the fold rise 14px again as they scroll into view (a scroll timeline; browsers without one simply show them).
PageEach page fades in over 200ms on arrival.
LinkThe link you clicked shows a 2px maroon line sweeping along its foot until its page arrives.
LoadingEvery page has a loading screen in its own shape and lead tone; placeholders shimmer with a soft sweep of light.
Less motionAll of it is switched off when the reader asks for reduced motion.
