One naming decision first. Today "company" means one of your ventures (Kilima Labs). A CRM also needs a word for the businesses you sell to and buy from. Proposal: your ventures stay companies, the people you deal with belong to organisations. The alternative is renaming your ventures to workspaces, which is a bigger change and more jargon.
